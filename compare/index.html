Tactics Board Apps, Compared Honestly

We make one of these, so read this with that in mind. We've still written it the way we'd want to read it — including the parts where another tool is the better buy. If you coach one sport at a high level, you'll probably finish this page choosing something else, and that's fine.

The short version

  • Coaching one sport seriously, especially football? TacticalPad has the deepest single-sport tooling and the professional track record. It's also the cheapest paid option on mobile.
  • Want roster, attendance and player evaluations in the same app? Tactico, Cupello and Coachbetter are team-management platforms with a board attached. We aren't.
  • Want the biggest ready-made drill library? CoachThem (4,000+) and YouCoach (2,000+) dwarf everyone, us included.
  • Running several sports, or several coaches, on a small budget? That's where CourtDraw is hard to beat — see the per-coach maths below.

Where the money actually differs: multiple coaches

Almost every tool in this category charges per coach. That's invisible when you're one person and brutal when you're a club. For ten coaching staff, over one year:

  • CourtDraw Club — €99 total, all ten seats included. About €10 per coach.
  • Tactico — $47.88 × 10 ≈ $479.
  • Cupello — $72 × 10 ≈ $720.
  • Coachbetter — $12.99/mo × 10 × 12 ≈ $1,559.

That gap is the single clearest reason to pick us, and it only applies if you actually have several coaches. One coach, one sport? The gap closes to nothing, and TacticalPad undercuts everyone.

Where we lose

Worth saying plainly, because you'll find out in week two anyway:

  • No roster, attendance or player evaluations. If you want one app that also tracks who turned up and how they're developing, Tactico or Cupello will serve you better. We're a board and a planner, not a team-management system.
  • Our free tier is one sport. The multi-sport range is the paid feature. If you want several sports for nothing, e2c Tactics is free and multi-sport.
  • Our library is 200+ plays, not 4,000. CoachThem and YouCoach have spent years building drill catalogues and it shows.
  • No professional pedigree. TacticalPad is used by national teams and has over a million downloads. We're much newer and much smaller.

Where we win

  • Sport coverage. 38+ courts including ones nobody else bothers with — hurling, korfball, padbol, beach handball, fistball. If you coach something outside the big five, you may not have another option.
  • Nothing to install. It opens in a browser, works offline once loaded, and installs to a home screen if you want it there. No app store, no desktop licence.
  • Club pricing. Covered above. For a multi-coach, multi-sport club it isn't close.
  • Drawing over video and photos. Useful when the thing you're explaining already happened.

How to choose in one minute

  1. Do you need roster and attendance in the same tool? Yes → Tactico or Cupello. Stop here.
  2. Do you coach one sport, seriously, and want maximum depth in it? Yes → TacticalPad.
  3. Do you mainly want a huge library of ready-made drills to run? Yes → CoachThem or YouCoach.
  4. Do you coach several sports, or work with several coaches, and mainly need to draw and share plays? Yes → that's us. Try it free, no card.

All of these are real products made by people trying to help coaches. There's no wrong answer here, only a wrong fit.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the best free tactics board app?

CourtDraw and e2c Tactics both run free in a browser; TacticalPad has a free tier too. Free boards are close to commoditised now — the differences that matter are sports covered, offline support, and what happens when you want to save and reuse plays.

Is CourtDraw a good TacticalPad alternative?

If you coach one sport at a high level, probably not — TacticalPad is deeper and cheaper on mobile. If you run several sports or several coaches, CourtDraw usually costs less and covers more ground.

Which is cheapest for a club with multiple coaches?

CourtDraw Club is €99/year for up to ten coaching staff — about €10 per coach. Per-seat competitors land several times higher at the same headcount.

Do I need roster and attendance features?

Only if you'll use them. If you already run team admin elsewhere, or you coach sessions rather than manage a squad, you're paying for software you won't open.

Can I draw tactics on video or photos?

CourtDraw includes video and photo overlay on Pro. DrawTactics annotates screenshots. Most basic free boards don't do either.
