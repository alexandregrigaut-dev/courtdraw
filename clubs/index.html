What a club gets that ten separate accounts don't

  • A shared play library. One coach saves a press-break; every coach sees it that moment. This is the difference between a club playbook and ten private ones.
  • Your kit colours on every board. Set the club's primary and secondary colours once, and every coach's players match — no per-coach setup.
  • Branded PDF exports. Session handouts go out with your club's logo and name, not ours.
  • Presentation mode. Full-screen with phase controls, built for a projector in a team meeting rather than a phone on the touchline.
  • An admin dashboard. Roster, activity and usage at a glance, so you can see which coaches have actually adopted it.
  • A six-character join code. Coaches add themselves. You aren't provisioning ten accounts by hand.

FAQ

Questions Directors Ask

How many coaches does a Club plan cover?

Up to ten coaching staff on one plan, €99 a year — about €10 per coach. They join with a six-character code rather than individual invitations.

Is Club cheaper than buying Pro for each coach?

Two annual Pro subscriptions already cost about the same as one Club plan, and Club covers ten coaches rather than two. From the second coach onwards it's the cheaper way to buy.

Does it work for a club running several sports?

Yes — 38+ sports on one board. A club running three or four programmes uses one tool instead of three subscriptions.

What does the club owner see that coaches don't?

An admin dashboard with roster, activity and usage, plus control of the shared library and club branding. Coaches see the shared plays, the club colours, and their own work.

What happens when the trial ends?

Cancel any time before day seven and nothing is charged. Plays already saved stay in each coach's account.
