# The app, login, success, club-admin and join-club are deliberately NOT listed
# here. They each carry <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, and a noindex tag

# robots.txt means Googlebot never loads the HTML, never sees the tag, and the
# URL can still be indexed on the strength of inbound links alone — the exact
# outcome the noindex was added to prevent. Letting them be crawled is what
# makes the noindex take effect. Do not add Disallow lines for pages that
# already carry a noindex tag.
